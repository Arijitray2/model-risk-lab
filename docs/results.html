2 · What the options market believes

Merton fitted to four real SPY chains (~90 days to expiry), OTM quotes, vega-weighted.

Fig 3 (interactive). Real SPY implied-vol skew (dots) vs the 4-parameter Merton fit (line) and the flat Black–Scholes smile (dashed). Switch dates; hover for exact values; drag to zoom.

3 · The desk under model risk

Fig 4 (interactive). One representative seed per scenario: realised P/L vs the edge the desk believes vs true expected edge. Hover to read the gap in dollars at any date.

Fig 6 (interactive). Loss probability vs quoted half-spread (log x). The matched desk clears the 5% bar at 8.8¢; the mispriced desk never gets there below $15.
